The number just sits there

Man this stock been edging 60 so long. I actually posted that. Then it touched 60 and I posted that also. Then I sat there like okay now what. Now the real question is how long it stays there.

I dont even know when I started checking it every day. One day it was just a ticker. Then it became a habit. Open the app. Look at the number. Close the app. Do this between meetings like it is work.

The loop

  • It is below. I wait.
  • It is near. I wait more.
  • It touches. I still wait.
  • Nothing in my life changed at 60.

I think I like the waiting more than the number. Same as a test match on 101 for 1. Same as one month till the next race. Something is about to happen so the day has a shape.

If it drops I will open the app again. If it stays I will open the app again. This is not advice. This is just what I do when the laptop is open and I am supposed to be looking busy.

P. V. Santosh
