This city is good until you have to go somewhere

I have been in bengaluru since last one year. I keep saying the city has a lot to offer. That is true if you are already there. The second you have to go somewhere the whole thing changes.

Honestly speaking, apart from the regular buzz and the hype around bars and cafes, I feel this city in itself has a lot to offer. I already wrote that. I still think it. Then I sit in traffic and review code on my phone because what else are you going to do.

Would be a joke if I said I like living in India with the dust going around, the irrepairable roads, never ending traffic, rains flooding, list just goes on. I posted that also. Both things can be true. That is the annoying part.

The ride

  • Leave early.
  • Still late.
  • Open the score.
  • Open the repo on the phone.
  • Reach office already tired.

Prestige tech park on a weekday evening is its own sport. I ran a 5K there once and that was the only time the place felt simple. Just a loop. No signal. No cab. No “I am 10 mins away” for 40 minutes.

Anyway. The city is good. Till you have to go somewhere.

P. V. Santosh
