Most days I just look busy

I put that line in my bio as a joke. Some days I write code. Most days I just look busy. Then I opened the bio later and it was just true.

Slack is green. Calendar has something. I am in the meeting. I am also checking the score. Software Engineer II sounds proper when you say it out loud. In the day it is more like this.

The actual day

  • Standup.
  • Review what the agent wrote.
  • Wait for someone.
  • Look at the terminal.
  • Look busy.

I am not even complaining. I like this work. I just dont know why we all pretend the whole day was deep work. Half of it is just being present so nobody thinks you vanished.

If I am honest a lot of the real thinking happens later. On the ride. In traffic. After gym. Not in the 11am call.

P. V. Santosh
