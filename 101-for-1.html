101 for 1 at lunch

I was supposed to be eating. Instead I was staring at 101 for 1 on Day 1. KL Rahul 32 off 77. Padikkal 35 off 49. Not the fastest start. But the foundation is there. I actually typed that and posted it like I was in the commentary box.

This is how a lot of my days go. Work on one side. Scorecard on the other. I tell myself I will just check at lunch and then lunch becomes the whole session.

Things I have said this year

  • Not the fastest start but the foundation is there.
  • Now let’s cash in during the second session.
  • Bazball era over??
  • A series to forget.
  • Thank you women team for making us forget the 4-0.

I dont even watch it properly sometimes. I just need to know the number. 101 for 1. That is enough to sit through the afternoon. If they collapse I will know in five minutes anyway. Someone will put it on X.

Anyway second session. Let’s cash in. I should eat also.
