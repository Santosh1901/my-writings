Eat lunch. Go break your legs. Repeat.

Some weekends are just this. Eat lunch. Go break your legs. Repeat. I posted two gym photos like that and it looked like a joke. It was also the plan.

Saturday I eat. Then I go. Then I come back and sit like I did something big. Sunday if there is no race I do it again. If there is a race the gym waits. I am not proud of that order. It is just the order.

After a quality session even the walk back feels different. So beautiful that it relaxes. I wrote that once about a photo. I still dont know if I meant the place or the body shutting up for a bit.

The weekend

  • Lunch.
  • Gym.
  • Eggs.
  • Score or race or both.
  • Sleep early for once.

Then Monday comes and I look busy again. The log looks clean for two days and I feel like I have a life. Then a week goes missing. Then I start again. That’s the thing about life. Not giving up each day no matter what. I told someone that on X. I should tell it to myself also.

P. V. Santosh
