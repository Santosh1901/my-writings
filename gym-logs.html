Gym logs when I dont flake

The bio also says gym logs when I dont flake. That second part is doing a lot of work.

I used to be 82. Now I am around 73. Took 6-7 months. I did not have some big plan. I just let it work on its own and then one day the jeans were loose and I thought okay this is happening.

Now that I got slim I go for weights thrice a week. Cardio in between. I ran after so long and the rate is slower but I am getting there. Eating eggs after gym hits different. That is not even a take. That is just true.

The flake part

  • Some weeks I go three times and feel like a different person.
  • Some weeks I open Zomato and the log is empty.
  • I keep waiting for that random Tuesday when I suddenly see the pump.
  • I asked fit people if they were foodies. Nobody had a clean answer.

I dont have a system. I have a week that is going okay and a week that is not. The log only exists on the okay weeks. Hence the bio.
