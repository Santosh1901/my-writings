One month till the next race

The race ended and I sat there like okay now what. Norris won. Max second. Antonelli third. Bad luck for Piastri. I typed all of that and then I typed one month till next race and closed the app.

I do this every Sunday. I tell myself I will not stay up. Then quali happens and I am already in it. Then the race happens and I am sending “clearly him right? guys??” to nobody.

Max for WDC. A man can hope.

The week after

  • Monday I am still thinking about the last stint.
  • Tuesday I am normal again.
  • Wednesday I check if any team radio dropped.
  • Then it is just work and gym till the next one.

I dont even know when this started. One day I was just a cricket guy. Then I watched a race and now my Sundays have a shape. When there is a break the Sunday feels empty. Like the day forgot to do something.

Well whatever. One month.

P. V. Santosh
