Gonna show my hometown barber this

I saw something on X and the first thought was I should show my hometown barber. Not a friend. Not the group chat. The barber. Nostalgic af. That was the whole tweet.

I dont even know why that came first. Maybe because that shop has not changed. Same chair. Same talk. Same “you have come after so long”. In bengaluru the salon is nicer and I still dont remember the guy’s name.

What I miss without saying I miss it

  • Someone cutting hair who already knows how you want it.
  • No app.
  • No “which package sir”.
  • Just sitting there listening to whatever is on the TV.

I left for a job and now I live in a city that is supposed to be better. It is better in a lot of ways. Work is here. Gym is here. The race is on at a decent hour. But some small things from home still sit in the head for no reason.

I will probably forget to show him. I will go home, sit in that chair, and talk about work like work is a place far away. Then I will come back here and remember the shop again when some random video shows up.

P. V. Santosh
